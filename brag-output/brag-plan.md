# Brag Plan: MathLab

## What is this app?
MathLab is an interactive mathematical laboratory that lets students and educators test ideas, visualize geometric relationships in 3D, manipulate formulas dynamically, and play mathematical strategy games.

## The angle
An ultra-fast teaser with hyper-dynamic cuts contrasting dry, monotonous textbook pages with MathLab's vibrant, real-time 3D geometry lab, dynamic formula sliders, and interactive math playground.

## Hook (0:00 - 0:05)
Rapid 1-second cuts flashing through dry textbook pages, then slamming them shut.
- Text overlay: "Math is boring?"
- Voiceover: "Think math is just dry formulas on a page?"
- Motion: Rapid cuts between monochromatic dense equation sheets, ending with an emphatic book slam impact.

## The Proof (0:05 - 0:15)
Fast-forward screen capture spinning an interactive 3D geometry shape in the 3D Shapes Lab and adjusting variables dynamically in the Formula Lab.
- Text overlay: "Visualize. Experiment. Master."
- Voiceover: "Welcome to MathLab. Visualize complex equations, jump into interactive 3D experiments, and master formulas dynamically."
- Motion: Kinetic rotation of a wireframe/shaded 3D cylinder & cone with live radius/height dimensions, followed by live formula variable slider shifts ($r$, $h$) with real-time recalculation of volume and surface area.

## The Flex (0:15 - 0:20)
A quick, sleek scroll highlighting the Math Playground challenges and Fun Facts section.
- Text overlay: "Complete Mathematical Sandbox"
- Voiceover: "From school basics to advanced university concepts, it's a complete sandbox."
- Motion: Smooth high-speed cascade of playground challenge cards (Tower of Hanoi, Rubik's Cube, Magic Squares, Nim) and mathematical fun facts.

## Call to Action (0:20 - 0:25)
Clean end card showing the URL mathlab.bolt.host in big, bold text with a sleek cursor clicking a "Get Started" button.
- Text overlay: "mathlab.bolt.host"
- Voiceover: "Go try it out right now at mathlab.bolt.host!"
- Motion: Glowing hero typography, primary cyan/blue glow button, simulated mouse cursor entering and clicking "Get Started" with ripple effect.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Moving from dry static paper equations to the dark-mode 3D Shapes Lab.
2. Key action: Interacting with 3D rotation controls and adjusting geometric parameters in real time.
3. Result: Exploring mathematical games and visiting mathlab.bolt.host.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic / fast-paced
- Creative direction: Ultra-fast 20-second teaser, hyper-dynamic cuts, focusing strictly on 3D shapes lab features, ending with a call to action directing viewers only to visit https://mathlab.bolt.host.
- Pacing: Snappy, high-energy transitions, bold typography, exact beat synchronization.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 5.0s (0.00s - 5.00s)
Monochrome, dry, high-density textbook page flashes every 1.0s. Suddenly slams shut with dust/shake. Bold red/white headline slams in: "Math is boring?"
Sequential cuts: Page 1 (0.0s - 1.2s), Page 2 (1.2s - 2.4s), Page 3 (2.4s - 3.5s), Slam shut & Text (3.5s - 5.0s).

### Scene 2 — The Proof (3D Shapes Lab & Formulas) — 10.0s (5.00s - 15.00s)
High-tech 3D laboratory environment. Dynamic interactive 3D rotating geometry (cylinder, cone, sphere) with coordinate rings, live dimension callouts ($r=3$, $h=5$, $V \approx 141.37$), followed by live Formula Lab parameter sliders adjusting and animating values in real time.

### Scene 3 — The Flex (Playground & Fun Facts) — 5.0s (15.00s - 20.00s)
Sleek cascade of playground puzzle badges (Tower of Hanoi, Rubik's Cube, Magic Squares, Nim) alongside glowing cards featuring fascinating mathematical fun facts.

### Scene 4 — Call to Action — 5.0s (20.00s - 25.00s)
Deep blue gradient background with ambient particle glow. Large typography: `mathlab.bolt.host`. An animated cursor glides in and clicks "Get Started", producing a bright button ripple and glow.
